Why there are two sources

Application entries are written by the observer pipeline and know which member of staff performed the action — something the database has no concept of. Database entries are written by the trg_appointment_audit and trg_invoice_payment_audit triggers and fire for any change to those tables, including one made over a SQL console or by a maintenance script. Together they answer both "who did this?" and "what changed?".
