Passwords are stored only as BCrypt hashes and are never displayed, logged or recoverable. Five consecutive failures lock an account for 15 minutes; the lock is time-boxed rather than permanent so that nobody can lock a colleague out of the clinic by deliberately failing their sign-in. A member of staff who leaves is deactivated, never deleted — their name must remain readable on the appointments they created.
